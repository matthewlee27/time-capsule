### music-in-a-bottle
The Spotify/last.fm time capsule has the goal of putting together a playlist that best captures a specific time period in your life. All you need to do is allow access to your last.fm listening data, specify a time period, and we will capture what that time looked like through music-in-a-bottle.

### driving thesis / goal
This project was inspired by my seasonal playlists that I make; the issue is, these seasonal playlists span 3 months and are a bit performative, and often I will stumble across songs that make me feel nostalgic. I'm also trying to steer away from LLM as a product (thus far) as I feel there is a lot of that, and can pure statistics without LLMs recover the same kinds of trends (or better?!) that LLMs might be able to? Anyhow, we explore two questions that relate to the relationship between music and time:

1. What does nostalgia look like, and how can we effectively measure it in the form of playlists?
2. Can we recover impactful life events from identifying key pivots in music activity?
